How to play

💧 Tap a tube to select it (it glows).

👆 Tap another tube to pour the top layer into it.

💡 Pour only onto an empty tube or one with the same color on top.

🌈 A tube is solved when it's one solid color, full.

⭐ Fewer pours = more stars!

↩️ Tap Undo to revert, Restart to reset.
